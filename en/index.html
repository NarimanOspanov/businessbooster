FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is GEO and how is it different from SEO?
SEO optimizes for search engines (Google, Bing). GEO — Generative Engine Optimization — optimizes for generative AI: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity. They don't show a list of links; they give one answer and recommend specific businesses. GEO gets you into those recommendations.
I only have Instagram, no website. Will it work?
Yes — that's one of our core scenarios. The agent studies your page — products, prices, contacts, reviews — and assembles a full bridge site that both people and AI understand.
I already have a website. What do you do then?
We don't duplicate it — that would hurt you. Instead we make your existing site AI-readable (structured data, server-rendered content) and build your presence where AI actually looks: Google & Bing business profiles, review platforms and AI shopping feeds. If you don't have a site, we create one on your own domain.
How fast will AI start recommending me?
The bridge site is published in minutes. Indexing and appearing in AI answers is gradual: first results usually show within a few weeks, and visibility keeps growing over time.
Won't the marketplace just integrate with AI itself?
It will — Etsy is already inside ChatGPT. But that puts the marketplace into AI, not you: orders stay anonymous, the commission stays, the customer stays theirs. We put your brand into AI — cited by name, paid directly, with the customer data yours. Keep the marketplace for volume; own the AI channel for margin and repeat buyers.
What is "Agent-to-Agent"?
Increasingly it's not a human who buys, but their AI assistant: it searches, compares, books. Agent-to-Agent is a protocol that lets a buyer's agent interact with your business agent directly: check availability, place an order, book a time slot.
