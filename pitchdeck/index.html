02 / 11
Problem

Search is moving to AI.
Local businesses are invisible there.

People ask ChatGPT "where do I order flowers tonight?" — and get one answer, not ten links. Businesses that aren't in that answer lose demand silently.

🕳️

Unreadable to LLMs

SMB sites are JavaScript-heavy with no structured data — and AI crawlers don't execute JS. Prices, hours and inventory are simply unreadable.

🚫

No way to transact

Even when AI finds a business, it can't check availability, book a slot or take payment. The deal dies in the dialogue.

📉

Silent traffic loss

Merchants see Google traffic decline but have no idea AI answers exist as a channel — or that competitors are already in them.

03 / 11
Solution

One link in → an AI-ready storefront + a business agent out

The merchant pastes a link to their marketplace store (Kaspi, Wildberries, Ozon), Instagram or website. Our agent builds a server-rendered storefront on the merchant's own domain, registers the business everywhere AI actually looks — shopping feeds, Google & Bing profiles, review platforms — and becomes its representative to every AI assistant.

Business model

SaaS today, take rate tomorrow

🆓

Free tier = funnel

Bridge site on a subdomain, basic GEO. Zero-friction onboarding: one link, no card.

💠

$19/mo Pro

A2A orders & bookings, daily sync, AI visibility analytics. Priced for SMBs, sold self-serve.

💳

1–2% transaction fee

As agent-to-agent commerce goes live, we take a small cut of every order and booking closed by the agents.

The subscription funds the land-grab; the take rate scales with the agent economy itself. At 10K Pro merchants that's $2.3M ARR before any transaction revenue.
